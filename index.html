What is Doc2Contact?

Doc2Contact is a contact scanner and document-to-contact app that extracts names and phone numbers from images and files using AI. Scan a handwritten contact list, business card, printed contact sheet, or upload a PDF, Excel, or Word file — then review and save the contacts directly to your phone. No need to type every contact manually.
